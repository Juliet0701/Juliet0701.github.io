Introduction
个人简介

Hi, I'm Tang Li Hang.
Passionate about various extracurricular activities such as reading, drawing, and listening to music, and occasionally ponders on the philosophy of life, but has not figured out any clear answers. Has good writing skills, is tall, and has a bold and unrestrained personality. Likes to experience various small things in life, pursues a peaceful life, occasional surprises are fine, but not fond of scares. Believes that details determine success or failure, so often spends a long time on things. Has average grades, not good at sports, but always a hardworking and upward student.

你好，我是陈立航。
热衷于各种学业外的事物，比如阅读、绘画、听歌之类的，偶尔也会思考人生哲理，当然也没思索出个所以然。文笔不错，高个子，豪放不羁。喜欢体验生活中的各种小事，向往平淡，偶尔的惊喜还可以，惊吓就免了。坚信细节决定成败，因此做事经常花费较长的时间。成绩中规中矩，运动不太行，但始终是个努力向上的普通学生。

My 9 Hobbies
我的爱好

Reading

阅读

阅读 Reading
I'm keen on reading books, including novels and comics. The most enjoying part of reading books is experiencing the emotions of characters through words on the page.

我喜欢阅读书籍，包括小说及漫画。阅读最享受的部分莫过于通过页面上的文字，去感受角色们的情感。

Writing

写作

写作 Writing
I've always believed that writing is the most romantic way to communicate with others, as it allows me to carefully consider my words and ensure that my message is conveyed exactly as I intend.

我一直都相信写作是最浪漫的沟通方式，因为我可以在写作时仔细思考我的文字，并确保我的文字的表达能正如我所想。

Gaming

游戏

游戏 Gaming
Playing mobile games doesn't just bring me entertainment, but also helps train my mind in problem-solving, money and time management, and creativity.

玩手游不仅仅是带给我娱乐，它还有训练我解决问题的思维，金钱及时间管理和培养我的创意。

Anime

看动漫

看动漫 Watching Anime
The main reason I love to watch anime is that it transports me to a world beyond reality, a world that I wish existed. Through watching anime, I can experience the joy of having close relationships with family, friends, and lovers, and also feel the pain of losing them all. This makes me cherish these relationships in real life even more.

我爱观看动漫，最主要的原因是它能让我去往一个超越现实的世界，一个我希望能存在的世界。通过观看动漫，我可以体验拥有亲情、友情和爱情的快乐，也可以感受失去它们的痛楚。这让我在现实生活中更加珍惜现有的关系。

Drawing

画画

画画 Drawing
I'm still an amateur at drawing, but I really enjoy it. The sense of satisfaction from starting with nothing, drawing step by step, and finally completing a piece is incomparable. Currently, my main focus is on drawing anime characters, but I know there is still a long way for me to master my skills.

我现在还是一位绘画的业余爱好者，不过我真的享受绘画的过程。那种从零开始，一步步画直到完成作品的满足感是无可匹敌的。现在，我主要是绘画动漫人物，但我知道我的绘画技术不止于此。

Chess

下棋

下棋 Chess
The best thing about playing chess is when I can find an opponent who is at the same level as me, and neither of us can dominate the other. Of course, I am not always the winner. Having wins and losses can keep me humble in victory and resilient in defeat. Reflecting on failures often leads to more progress than just celebrating victories.

下棋时最好的事是当我能对上一位和我旗鼓相当，不相上下的对手。当然，我不总是胜利的一方。下棋有输有赢，才能让我胜而不骄，败而不馁。在失败中反省总是比胜利后雀跃进步得多。

Music

音乐

音乐 Music
Friedrich Nietzsche once said, "Without music, life would be a mistake." Music is a refreshing breeze that can dispel the troubles in one's heart; music is a kind of attitude that can add fun to life; music is a monologue that can express all sorts of human emotions. I enjoy listening to music in various languages, including but not limited to Chinese, English, and Japanese songs.

尼采曾经说过：“没有音乐，生命将是一个错误。”音乐是一阵清风，能吹散心中烦恼；音乐是一种态度，能增添生活趣味；音乐是一段独白，能说尽人间百态。我喜欢听各种语言的音乐，包括但不限于华语、英语及日语歌等。

Sports

运动

运动 Sports
Despite never won any prize on Sports Day or at the sports competitions, I'm actually passionate to play any kind of sports. My favourite sport is football. I started playing football since primary school, but due to venue limitations, I gradually stopped playing football after entering secondary school. Nevertheless, I still often invite my friends to play badminton during school holiday.

虽然从未在运动会以及体育比赛中获得奖项，但我其实对各种体育都非常有热诚。我最喜欢的体育是足球。我从小学便开始踢足球，但是由于场地限制，在上中学后，我便渐渐没踢了。虽然如此，我仍经常邀约我的朋友们在学校假期间打羽球。

Languages

学习外语

学习外语 Learning Languages
Language is the basic bridge for human communication, and also the cornerstone for the continuation of human civilisation. Although I can use English, Mandarin, Malay, and Cantonese dialects fluently, it never hurts to learn more languages. Influenced by Japanese anime, I am currently learning Japanese, hoping that it can complement my language skills.

语言是人类沟通的基本桥梁，也是人类文明延续的基石。尽管已经可以正常使用英语、华语、马来语及广东方言，但学习多种语言总不是坏事。受到日本动漫的影响，因此我现在正学着日语，期待可以相辅相成。
